Daily Games Hub

Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026. Daily Games Hub is a browser extension that lists third-party daily games and keeps a local record of results you choose to enter.

What is stored, and where

The extension uses browser.storage.local in your browser profile to store:

  • results you enter (game, date, optional variant, outcome, optional guesses or score, and notes);
  • your favorite game IDs and daily-routine game IDs;
  • small display preferences such as sort order, routine filter and activity window.

This data stays on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to any analytics, advertising, or cloud service. The extension has no account system and no backend.

What is not collected

The extension does not read page contents, cookies, passwords, search history or answers from game sites, and it never reads your clipboard automatically. If you choose to paste Wordle or Connections share text into the import dialog, that text is parsed locally and is not stored; only the resulting record is kept. There are no content scripts and opening a game is never tracked as a completed play.

Permissions

  • storage — save your results, favorites and preferences locally.

The Firefox sidebar is declared directly in the extension manifest; it is not a runtime permission and does not grant access to web pages. No host permissions or content scripts are requested.

Opening a game navigates to a third-party website in a normal browser tab. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies. This extension does not embed or proxy third-party games.

Backups

You can export a JSON backup from Settings. Export happens only when you click the button. Importing a backup is validated in the extension before it is merged or used to replace local data. Resetting removes local results, favorites, your routine and preferences from this browser.

Data sharing and sale

No personal data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. There are no ads and no analytics SDKs in the extension.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to https://github.com/Helloworld9099/daily-games-hub-privacy/issues.
